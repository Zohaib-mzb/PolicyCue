Northstar Market — controlled commerce policy, revision 1
Refund eligibility. Unused physical goods may be returned if they remain in their original packaging with all supplied accessories. Opening the outer delivery carton alone does not make a product used. Personalized goods and downloaded digital products are excluded from change-of-mind refunds, even if the buyer has not used them. A physical product with a manufacturing defect is assessed under the damaged-item process rather than the change-of-mind rules. Staff must not treat a bank dispute as an approved return. Customers should keep their receipt until the return is resolved. These eligibility rules describe which purchases can be returned; the request window is specified separately.

Refund request window. A customer seeking a change-of-mind refund must submit the request within 30 calendar days after delivery, not after the order was placed. The request must include the order number and the item being returned. Customer support records the submission date when the completed form reaches the returns portal. Receiving an automated receipt does not establish eligibility. After approval, support supplies a return label and packing instructions. A parcel may arrive at the warehouse later without invalidating a request that was submitted on time. The customer remains responsible for protecting the contents in transit and keeping the parcel tracking receipt until the warehouse confirms arrival.

Refund processing. Once the warehouse has inspected and accepted a returned item, the approved refund is sent to the original payment method within seven business days. The processing clock starts on acceptance after inspection, not on delivery to the customer or submission of the return request. Store credit is not substituted without the customer's agreement. Support sends a confirmation when the refund is issued; a payment provider may display the credit later. Original express-delivery fees are not refunded for a change-of-mind return. A rejected inspection is explained in writing, with photographs where useful, and the customer may ask support to review that decision before the item is sent back.

Exchanges. A size exchange for an unused garment may be requested within 21 calendar days after delivery, provided the desired size is in stock. The buyer must return the original garment with its tags attached. An exchange replaces the garment; it does not restart the original refund request window. If the requested size is unavailable, support can offer the normal refund process if that process's conditions are met. A colour preference is handled as a return and a new purchase, rather than a size exchange. Exchange postage within the domestic service area is covered once per order. Further exchanges require a fresh support assessment, and the policy does not promise stock availability during busy periods.

Domestic delivery. Standard domestic shipping normally takes three to five business days after dispatch. Orders may spend up to two business days in preparation before dispatch, so a delivery estimate is not measured from checkout. Express shipping normally takes one to two business days after dispatch. Both services provide parcel tracking when the carrier receives the package. A delivery address correction should be requested before dispatch; after dispatch, the carrier decides whether rerouting is possible. Preparation and transit estimates are separate from refund or exchange deadlines. Weather disruption may extend the delivery estimate, and support will help trace a delayed parcel rather than silently cancel the order.

International delivery. International orders are accepted only for destinations listed at checkout. Shipping charges are displayed before payment, and this policy does not offer free international shipping. Import duties and local customs charges are paid by the recipient. Standard international transit is normally eight to fifteen business days after dispatch, excluding time held by customs. A customer should check the recipient's contact details before placing an order because customs may require additional information. Refusing to pay a customs charge does not automatically create a refund entitlement. Where a parcel returns to the warehouse, support reviews its condition and the applicable return rules before proposing a resolution.

Subscription cancellation. Monthly refill subscriptions can be cancelled in the account dashboard. To prevent the next renewal charge, cancellation must be completed at least 48 hours before the scheduled renewal time. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not automatically refund a refill order that has already been charged. A charged physical refill is assessed under the same return eligibility and request rules as other physical goods. Pausing a subscription skips one renewal and leaves later renewals active; it is different from cancellation. Support can help locate the dashboard but cannot guarantee that a message sent near the cutoff will be processed before renewal. A confirmation email records the cancellation time.

Damaged or defective arrivals. Customers should report an item that arrived damaged or with a manufacturing defect within 14 calendar days after delivery, attaching the order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging. Support first reviews whether replacement parts or a replacement item can resolve the problem. Where a return is necessary, Northstar supplies prepaid postage for the defective item. The unused-item restriction for change-of-mind returns does not prevent a defect claim after reasonable inspection. Damage caused by customer misuse is not covered by this process. A claim is assessed against the reported defect; a scratch to the delivery carton without damage to the goods is not itself a product defect.
