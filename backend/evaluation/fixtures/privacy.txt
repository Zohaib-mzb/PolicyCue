Harbor Workspace — controlled privacy policy, revision 1
Account information. Harbor collects an account email address, display name and workspace membership details to operate shared workspaces. The email address is used for security notices and service communications, while a display name appears beside a member's contributions. The account form does not require a home address. Optional profile photographs can be removed without closing the account. Workspace administrators can see members' display names and membership roles but cannot see another member's private support tickets. Account records are distinct from diagnostic events generated during use of the service. Providing an optional profile field does not automatically enroll a member in marketing messages.

Diagnostic information. The service records device type, browser version, time of access and error codes to diagnose reliability problems. Raw diagnostic events are retained for 30 days and then deleted or converted into aggregate statistics that cannot identify an account. The service does not use the content of private documents in diagnostic event messages. Error reports submitted voluntarily may include an attachment, which the member should review before sending. Troubleshooting access is limited to staff working on the reported incident and is recorded for audit. A short-term diagnostic event should not be confused with the separately retained support conversation about the incident or with a financial transaction record.

Support ticket retention. Support tickets, including attachments sent with the ticket, are retained for 180 days after the ticket is closed. Closing a ticket means support has marked the conversation resolved; the retention clock does not begin when the first message is sent. If a member reopens a closed ticket before deletion, the period is measured from its later closure. Members should avoid placing passwords or payment-card numbers in a ticket. Support staff may remove accidentally submitted secrets earlier than the scheduled deletion date. Ticket content is used to resolve requests and review service quality, not to create public case studies identifying the member without separate permission.

Billing records. Invoices and transaction identifiers are retained for six years after the end of the financial year in which the transaction occurred. This retention rule applies even when a member closes an account or requests deletion. Harbor stores payment status and the last four digits supplied by its payment processor; the processor handles full card details. Billing records are used for accounting, disputes and required financial records, rather than personalised advertising. A workspace administrator may download invoices from the billing console while the workspace remains active. Removing an optional profile photograph has no effect on the retention schedule for invoices or transaction identifiers.

Account deletion. A member can request account deletion through the privacy form after verifying control of the account email address. Harbor removes the active account profile within 30 days of a verified request. Records subject to the billing-retention rule are kept separately and are not erased by this profile-deletion process. Deletion of an account is not the same as removing a person from one workspace, because a person may belong to several workspaces. The confirmation identifies when profile deletion is completed. Members who need a copy of their contributions should request an export before deletion, since the privacy team cannot restore an erased active profile merely to satisfy a later export request.

Service providers. Harbor shares information with contracted hosting, customer-support and payment service providers only as needed to deliver those functions. Providers are instructed to process that information on Harbor's behalf and may not use it for their own advertising. Harbor does not sell account email addresses. Access to a support attachment is limited to the staff and providers needed to investigate that request. A workspace administrator inviting a member is not treated as a purchase of that member's personal data. This section explains categories of recipients and restrictions on their use of information; it does not identify the physical country in which hosting infrastructure is located.

Marketing choices. Product-news emails are sent only when a member has opted in. A member may withdraw that choice through the unsubscribe link, and Harbor applies the change within two business days. Unsubscribing from product news does not stop security alerts, password-reset messages or notices needed to operate an existing account. Marketing preference changes are not account-deletion requests. Staff must check the recorded preference before sending a new campaign rather than assume that membership in a paid workspace implies consent. A request to correct an email address should be made through the account settings so future service notices reach the verified address.

Access and correction. After verifying account control, Harbor responds to an export request within 20 business days and provides account profile data and the member's own contributions in a downloadable archive. The export does not include other members' private support tickets or internal security investigation notes. Members may correct their display name directly in settings; changing the account email requires verification of the new address. An export request alone does not close an account or remove workspace access. If verification cannot be completed, the privacy team asks for additional account information rather than send the archive to an unverified destination. These procedures protect against someone impersonating another member.
